01About

I'm a senior full-stack engineer with 10+ years of production experience at MyAtOnce, a B2B e-commerce SaaS company. For a period, I was the company's sole engineer, responsible for databases, DevOps, server infrastructure, application development, and production support. I remain its only US engineer and a senior technical resource for the distributed development team.

My work spans GCP, MySQL, PHP/Slim APIs, and React and Next.js frontends. I've delivered ERP integrations and led the response to a production security incident. Our clients range from small businesses to large organizations, so I adapt integrations to widely varying data volumes, formats, and business rules.

I came to software through startup web development and Coding Dojo's full-stack immersive, where I earned the Black Belt. I enjoy learning by building: my open-source MCP Photoshop Server brings image editing and AI generation into coding-assistant workflows. Across my work, I value reliable systems and clear communication with the people who depend on them.
